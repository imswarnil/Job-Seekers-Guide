There is one person behind this site, and you are writing to him rather than to
a ticketing system.

## Write to me at imswarnil.com

Everything personal now goes through my own site:
**[imswarnil.com](https://imswarnil.com)**. That is where I write, where the
contact details live, and where a message actually reaches me. Questions about
your own path, anything private, a privacy request: start there.

## A mistake in a lesson

Wrong code, a broken link, an explanation that does not hold up.
[Open an issue](https://github.com/imswarnil/job-seekers-guide/issues) or use
the "Report a mistake" link at the bottom of any lesson. This is the fastest
route to an actual fix, because it lands next to the file that is wrong. Every
page also has an "Edit this page" link that opens a pull request, and those are
welcome.

## What I cannot help with

I cannot refer you to a company, get you an interview, or promise you a job,
and I would not trust anybody who does. The [terms](/terms) say why.

I write this around a full-time job, so expect days rather than hours.
[The longer version of who I am is here](/my-story), and the rest of the story
carries on at [imswarnil.com](https://imswarnil.com).
