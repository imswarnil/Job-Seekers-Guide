These are the right fifty. Every problem below is a well-known one under its
common name, so you can find it on any practice site (LeetCode, GeeksforGeeks,
HackerRank, Coding Ninjas). Each is tied to the lesson in this track that
teaches the pattern, so when one beats you, you know exactly what to reread.

::note
This list is what I would give a friend preparing for fresher and 0–3 year roles,
not a guarantee of what any company asks. Service company tests lean on the easy
ones; product company rounds on the mediums. The two hards are there so the first
hard problem you meet in an interview is not the first you have ever seen.
::

## How to use this list

- **Order:** top to bottom. Each group builds on the ones above it.
- **Pace:** about six or seven a week alongside the lessons, which is the
  eight-week plan below.
- **Rule:** 25 minutes on an easy, 40 on a medium, 60 on a hard. Stuck at the
  limit? Read the approach only, close the page, write it yourself.
- **Track it:** a spreadsheet with columns *problem, date, solved alone (yes /
  hint / no), time taken, pattern, revisit on*. Anything that is not "yes" gets
  a revisit date three days later.

## An eight-week order

::timeline{label="Fifty problems in eight weeks"}
  :::timeline-item{date="Week 1" title="Arrays and strings" state="todo"}
  Problems 1–9. Get fast at loops, indices and `StringBuilder`.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{date="Week 2" title="Hashing and two pointers" state="todo"}
  Problems 10–17. The two patterns behind most easy and medium questions.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{date="Week 3" title="Windows and binary search" state="todo"}
  Problems 18–24. Do 20 and 24 last, and allow an hour each.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{date="Week 4" title="Sorting and recursion" state="todo"}
  Problems 25–30. Draw the recursion tree for 29 and 30 on paper.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{date="Week 5" title="Linked lists, stacks and queues" state="todo"}
  Problems 31–38. Draw the pointers before every line of code.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{date="Week 6" title="Trees and heaps" state="todo"}
  Problems 39–44.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{date="Week 7" title="Graphs, DP and greedy" state="todo"}
  Problems 45–50. Draw every DP table by hand first.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{date="Week 8" title="Revisit" state="todo"}
  Every problem marked "hint" or "no", timed, out loud, as if in the room.
  :::
::

::tip
After the fifty, do not jump to another list of three hundred. Take a timed
online assessment on any practice site once a week and redo the problems you
failed. Being able to solve fifty patterns cold beats having seen three hundred
once.
::
