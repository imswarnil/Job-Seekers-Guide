The maths section is handled. The same paper has a reasoning section, and it
tests something different: not calculation, but whether you read carefully and
draw before you think.

## Why reasoning is its own track

People who are good at maths lose marks here, and people who are weak at maths
often score well. The questions need almost no arithmetic. What they need is a
habit: put the information on paper in a shape, then read the answer off the
shape.

When I started practising in 2018, reasoning was the section where I improved
fastest. Seating arrangements went from "skip every time" to "attempt first"
in about two weeks, and the only change was that I stopped trying to hold the
arrangement in my head.

::tip
Every page in this track has a drawing method. Use it even when the question
looks easy. The easy-looking ones are where a missed "not" or "left of"
costs you.
::
