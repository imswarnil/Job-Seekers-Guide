Numbers and reasoning are handled. The last section of the paper is English,
and for me it was the hardest, because I did not think of it as something that
could be studied.

## The section that beat me

I grew up in Mahroni, a small town in Uttar Pradesh. At LNCT I could read
English well enough to pass exams, but I could not speak for two minutes in
English without stopping, and in the written round I answered grammar
questions by what "sounded right". What sounded right to me was often wrong.

In Bangalore I treated English the way I treated aptitude: a set of rules and
question types, practised every day. Grammar took about two weeks to become
reliable. Speaking took longer, and I will be honest about how long in the
speaking chapter.

::note
Verbal ability is the section most freshers do not prepare for, because they
assume they either know English or they do not. It is as learnable as
percentages. The questions are built on a small number of rules, and the rules
are on these pages.
::

## What is in this track

::steps
### Start here
How the verbal section is tested, company by company.

### Grammar
Tenses, articles and prepositions, subject-verb agreement, modals, and voice
and speech.

### The question types
Error spotting, sentence correction, fill in the blanks, sentence completion,
cloze tests, para jumbles and reading comprehension.

### Vocabulary
A method for learning words, then synonyms and antonyms, one-word substitution,
and idioms and phrases.

### Writing
Email writing and essay writing, for the papers that include them.

### Speaking
Spoken English for interviews, and the fear of speaking.

### The plan
Thirty days, from the first grammar rule to a full timed section.
::
