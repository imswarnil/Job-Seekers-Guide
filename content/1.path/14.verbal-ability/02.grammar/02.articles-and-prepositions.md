Tenses fixed the verb. The two smallest word groups in English, articles and
prepositions, carry a surprising share of the marks, because a blank in the
middle of a sentence is most often one of them.

## Why it matters

Fill in the blanks, error spotting and cloze tests lean heavily on these two.
In TCS NQT, Accenture, Cognizant, Capgemini and AMCAT-style papers I would
expect two to four questions per section to turn on an article or a
preposition. Check the current pattern.

They also show in every email you write. "I am writing regarding to the
vacancy" and "I have applied for the post in your company on Monday" are both
wrong, and both are sentences markers see all the time.

| # | Topic | Priority |

| 4 | At, on, in for time and place | High |

| 6 | Fixed pairs: verb or adjective + preposition | High |
| 7 | Words that take no preposition (discuss, enter, reach) | Medium |

## Articles: the rules

```text
A / AN depends on the first SOUND, not the first letter
  an hour, an honest man, an MBA, an FIR      (vowel sounds)
  a university, a European, a one-rupee coin  (consonant sounds: "yoo", "wun")

THE when the reader knows which one you mean
  I went to a walk-in. The walk-in was in Whitefield.   (second mention)
  the sun, the Earth, the internet                      (only one)
  the best, the first, the only                         (superlatives, order)
  the Ganga, the Himalayas, the Indian Ocean            (rivers, ranges, oceans)
  the poor, the unemployed                              (a group described by an adjective)

NO ARTICLE
  Students need practice.        (general plural, uncountable)
  He is in college / in hospital / at school   (the institution's purpose)
  Bangalore, India, Mount Everest              (most single names)
```

## Prepositions: the rules

| Use | At | On | In |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Time** | a clock time: at 9 am, at noon, at night | a day or date: on Monday, on 5 June | a month, year, season, part of the day: in June, in 2018, in the morning |
| **Place** | a point: at the gate, at the venue | a surface: on the table, on the second floor | inside an area: in the hall, in Bangalore |

```text
since + point in time      since Monday
for + length of time       for two weeks
during + a named period    during the interview
by = not later than        submit it by Friday
until = up to that time    wait until Friday

Fixed pairs that the papers love:
  interested in       good at            afraid of
  capable of          responsible for    married to
  different from      prefer X to Y      superior / inferior to
  apply for (a job)   apply to (a company)
  congratulate on     comply with        depend on
  insist on           consist of         agree with (a person) / to (a proposal)

No preposition after:
  discuss the plan   (NOT discuss about)
  enter the room     (NOT enter into, for a place)
  reach the venue    (NOT reach to)
  request a transfer (NOT request for, as a verb)
  regarding the job  (NOT regarding to)
```

::tip
Keep a page in your notebook headed "fixed pairs". Every time a question
catches you, the pair goes on the page. After two weeks that page is worth more
than any list you could download.
::

## A worked example

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="Article by sound" icon="i-lucide-type"}
  *He has ____ MBA from ____ university in Pune.*

  "MBA" starts with the sound "em", a vowel sound. "University" starts with
  the sound "yoo", a consonant sound.

  **Answer: an, a.**
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="Time prepositions" icon="i-lucide-calendar"}
  *The walk-in is ____ Saturday ____ 10 am ____ the Electronic City campus.*

  A day takes "on", a clock time takes "at", a point location takes "at".

  **Answer: on, at, at.**
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="The extra preposition" icon="i-lucide-search"}
  *We discussed about the offer (A) / and decided (B) / to accept it (C) / before
  Friday. (D)*

  **Error in A.** "Discuss" takes its object directly: "We discussed the offer".
  :::
::

## Common mistakes

- **"A university", not "an university".** Sound, not spelling.
- **"The" before a general plural.** "The students need practice" means
  particular students; "students need practice" means students in general.
- **"On" with a month, "in" with a date.** On 5 June, in June.
- **Extra prepositions:** discuss about, reach to, regarding to, return back.
- **"Since" with a length.** Since two years is wrong; for two years.

## How to practise

- **Days one and two:** learn both rule blocks. Say the a/an examples aloud;
  the sound is the rule.
- **Days three and four:** 15 questions a day from IndiaBix "Sentence
  Correction" filtered by eye for articles and prepositions, and the
  prepositions chapter of R.S. Aggarwal's *Objective General English*. Target
  **20 seconds** each.
- **Ongoing:** the fixed-pairs page, read once a day for a minute.

## Practice questions

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="1. Fill in: 'It took me ____ hour to find ____ venue.'"}
  **An, the.** "Hour" has a silent h. "The venue" is a particular one.
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="2. Fill in: 'I am good ____ quant but afraid ____ para jumbles.'"}
  **At, of.**
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="3. Correct it: 'I reached to the office on 9 am.'"}
  **I reached the office at 9 am.**
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="4. Correct it: 'She is the one of the best candidate.'"}
  **She is one of the best candidates.** "One of" takes a plural, and needs no
  "the" before it here.
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="5. Fill in: 'Please submit the form ____ Friday.' (by / until)"}
  **By.** Not later than Friday. "Until" would mean keep submitting up to Friday.
  :::
::
