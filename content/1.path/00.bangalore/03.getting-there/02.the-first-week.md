You are off the train with a suitcase and a folder. This page is the first seven
days as a checklist, so that by the end of the week you have a bed, a number, a
bank account and a route, and you can start studying.

## Why it matters

The first week sets the pattern. If you spend it lost, in an expensive lodge,
with no SIM and no plan, you will spend the second week recovering. If you spend
it on the checklist below, you will be sitting in a demo class by day eight.

It is also the week you are most likely to be cheated, because you do not yet
know what anything should cost. Go slowly with money.

## What to do

::steps
### Before you arrive: a bed for the first nights
Do not arrive without somewhere to sleep. Either a friend's room, or a PG that
takes a daily or weekly guest (many do), booked by phone. A cheap lodge near the
station is a fallback, not a plan. Tell the owner your arrival time.

### Day 1: arrive, eat, sleep
Get from the station to your bed by bus or metro, not by a tout's taxi. Eat.
Message home. Sleep. Do not try to do anything else.

### Day 2: a SIM card
Take your Aadhaar to an official store of any major mobile operator. Ask for a
local prepaid SIM with a data plan. If your home SIM works in Bangalore on
roaming, you can keep it and skip this, but a local number helps with PG owners
and HR calls.

### Day 2 or 3: look at PGs
Walk the area. Visit at least four. Use the checklist in the *Finding a PG*
lesson. Do not pay a deposit on the first one you see.

### Day 3 or 4: a bank account
If your bank has a branch in Bangalore, update your address or use it as it is.
If not, open a basic savings account at a nationalised or large private bank
near your PG. Take Aadhaar, PAN and photographs. You will want UPI working from
day one.

### Day 4 or 5: move into your PG
Pay the deposit and the first month only after the checks. Get a receipt for
both, with the amounts and the owner's name.

### Day 5 or 6: the route
Find the institutes near you. Walk or take the bus to each one at the time a
morning batch would start, so you see the real journey.

### Day 7: rest, and plan week two
Write down the demo classes you will sit next week. Call home.
::

## The checklist

Print this, or copy it into your notebook.

| Item | Done by | What you need | Rough cost when I went |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A bed for the first nights | Before you leave | A phone number and an address | ₹300 to ₹600 a night for a guest bed |
| Local SIM | Day 2 | Aadhaar, a photograph | A few hundred rupees with a plan |
| PG visits | Day 2 to 4 | The PG checklist, a notebook | Nothing |
| Bank account and UPI | Day 3 to 4 | Aadhaar, PAN, photographs | Nothing to open a basic account |
| PG deposit and first month | Day 4 to 5 | Money, and a receipt | One to two months' rent |
| Route to the institute | Day 5 to 6 | Google Maps, a bus pass | Bus fare |
| Demo class list | Day 7 | A list of institutes nearby | Nothing |

Prices change. These are what I remember; check today's.

## Getting around in week one

::feature-list{columns="2"}
:::feature{icon="i-lucide-bus" title="BMTC buses"}
The city buses go everywhere and are the cheapest way to move. Ask the
conductor if you are not sure; people will help. Google Maps shows bus routes.
:::
:::feature{icon="i-lucide-train-front" title="Namma Metro"}
The metro is faster and more predictable than a bus in traffic. Buy a smart card
if you will use it daily. The network has grown a lot since I went, so check the
current map.
:::
:::feature{icon="i-lucide-car" title="Autos"}
Use an app, or agree the fare before you get in. At the station, walk away from
the touts to the proper stand or book on the app.
:::
:::feature{icon="i-lucide-footprints" title="Walking"}
In your own area, walk. It is how you find the cheap mess, the photocopy shop
and the PG with the sign that is not on any website.
:::
::

## Staying safe

::warning
In the first week you do not know what is normal, and some people make money
from that.

- Do not pay anybody who offers to "arrange" a PG, a job or a SIM for a fee.
- Do not hand your Aadhaar or originals to a stranger. Show them, take them
  back.
- Do not share OTPs with anyone, ever, even someone claiming to be from your
  bank.
- Keep your emergency money in the bank, not in the suitcase.
::

## Common mistakes

- **Arriving with nowhere to sleep.** You will take the first room anybody
  offers, at any price.
- **Paying a PG deposit on day one.** You have not seen the others. Wait.
- **Starting an institute without a demo.** You will read in the *How to learn*
  chapter why three days of demos changed everything for me.
- **Trying to do everything on day one.** You have been on a train for a day
  and a half. Sleep.

## How to practise

On the train, write your own seven-day plan in your notebook using the table
above. Put a name, an address or a time against every line you can. The ones
you cannot fill are the ones to ask a friend in Bangalore about before you
arrive.
