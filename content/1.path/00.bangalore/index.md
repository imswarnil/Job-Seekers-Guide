In 2018 I finished engineering, went home to Mahroni, and had no idea what came
next. I had a CSE degree from LNCT Bhopal and I could not clear a written round.
A year later I had a job in Bangalore. This track is everything that happened in
between, except the studying, which is the rest of the guide.

## What this track is

It is not a course. Nothing in it is examined. It is a set of decisions and
checklists, in the order you will face them, from the conversation at home to
the day you sign an offer letter.

::note
Every price on these pages is **what it was when I went, in 2018 and 2019**,
unless it says otherwise. Rents and fees have gone up since. Where I give a
"today" figure it is a rough guide, and you should check it yourself before you
plan around it.
::

## Who this is for

If you have a degree, no job, a family that is worried, and a feeling that you
are behind everybody else, this is for you. That was me. You do not need to be
good at anything yet. You need a plan and the patience to follow it for a year.
