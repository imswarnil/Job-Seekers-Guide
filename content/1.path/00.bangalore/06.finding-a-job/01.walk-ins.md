The timetable says "walk-in" for the first time. This page is what a walk-in
drive is, where to find them, what to carry, and what the day is actually like.
I went to 33 of them. I was selected at the 34th.

## Why it matters

A **walk-in drive** (usually called a walk-in) is a hiring day. A company
announces a date, a time and an address, and anyone who meets the criteria can
turn up with a resume, without an appointment or a prior application. On the day
they run several rounds, one after another, and reject people at each one. Some
people leave with an offer, or a call for a final round, the same day.

For a fresher with no contacts and a thin resume, walk-ins are the most direct
route there is. Nobody filters your resume out before you get in the door. You
get to sit the test.

::pull-quote
At the 34th walk-in there were somewhere between 700 and 1000 of us. I was the
one they selected. The 33 before it were how I got good enough to be that one.
::

## Where to find them

| Source | What to do | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Naukri** | Search "walk-in" plus your skill and "Bangalore", filter by fresher and date | The largest source. Set up a daily alert |
| **LinkedIn** | Follow companies and HR people who post drives; search posts for "walk-in" | Many drives are announced in posts, not in the jobs section |
| **Freshersworld** | Fresher-only listings, including walk-ins | Built for exactly this |
| **Institute placement cell** | Check the board and the placement WhatsApp group daily | Often the drives most likely to take institute students |
| **WhatsApp and Telegram groups** | Join the groups your PG and institute friends are in | Fast, but full of forwards. Verify every one |
| **Company career pages** | Check the careers pages of companies you want | The most reliable; if a drive is on the official page, it is real |

::warning
Always check a walk-in on a second source before you travel across the city for
it: the company's own website or LinkedIn page. Fake drives exist, and some of
them end with a request for money. The next page covers the scams.
::

## What to carry

::steps
### Resumes
Five printed copies, on clean paper, in a folder. Some rounds hand your resume
to a new interviewer each time.

### The documents folder
Originals and photocopies of marksheets, degree, Aadhaar and PAN, plus
passport-size photographs. Many drives check documents before the written round.

### The call letter or listing
A screenshot or printout of the drive details, the reference number if there is
one, and the HR contact's name.

### Pens, water, food
Two pens. A bottle of water. Something to eat; days can run from morning until
evening and you may not want to leave the queue.

### Formal clothes, and the right shoes
You may be standing for hours before anything starts.
::

## The day itself

::timeline{label="A typical walk-in day, from my experience"}
:::timeline-item{title="Arrive early" date="Before the listed time" state="done"}
Earlier than you think. By the listed start time there can be hundreds of
people ahead of you in the queue.
:::
:::timeline-item{title="Registration and documents" date="Morning" state="done"}
A form, a token number, and a check of your documents and resume. Some people
are sent home here for missing papers or not meeting the percentage cut-off.
:::
:::timeline-item{title="The written round" date="Late morning" state="current"}
Aptitude, reasoning, verbal and sometimes basic technical questions. This is
where most of the room is rejected. It was where I was rejected most.
:::
:::timeline-item{title="Technical round" date="Afternoon" state="todo"}
One or two interviews: Java, OOPs, SQL, DBMS, a program on paper. Sometimes a
coding test on a computer instead.
:::
:::timeline-item{title="HR round" date="Afternoon or evening" state="todo"}
Tell me about yourself, why this company, salary, location, bond, joining date.
:::
:::timeline-item{title="Result" date="Same day or later" state="todo"}
Sometimes that evening, sometimes a call days later, sometimes nothing at all.
:::
::

::story-media{kind="image" label="The queue outside a walk-in drive in Bangalore, 2019"}
Hundreds of people in formals, folders in hand, before the doors opened.
::

Every company runs it differently. Some do the written round online on a
different day. Some do everything in one room in two hours. The shape above is
the common one.

## What to do after every walk-in

This is the habit that turned 33 rejections into one selection.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-notebook-pen"}
The same evening, write down:

1. Which round you reached.
2. Which questions or topics you could not answer, as exactly as you can
   remember them.
3. One thing to practise this week because of it.

After five walk-ins, read the notes back. You will see the same round and the
same kind of question rejecting you. That is your study plan.
::

## A worked example

What a rejection log looks like after a few weeks:

| Walk-in | Reached | What beat me | What I practised next |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Written | Ran out of time, half the paper untouched | Timed aptitude every morning |
| 2 | Written | Time and work, seating arrangement | Those two topics for a week |
| 3 | Technical | Could not explain the difference between an interface and an abstract class | OOPs, out loud |
| 4 | Written | Reading comprehension | English passages, timed |
| 5 | HR | Froze on tell me about yourself | Wrote it, recorded it, fixed it |

This is an illustration of the pattern, not a copy of my notebook, but mine
looked very like it: the written round again and again at first, then later
rounds as the written round stopped being the problem.

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting until you feel ready.** You never will. The walk-in tells you what
  to study.
- **Arriving at the listed time.** Arrive well before it.
- **Going to every drive in the city.** Two or three good ones a week, with
  time to study between them, beats five a week exhausted.
- **Not writing anything down afterwards.** Then the rejection taught you
  nothing.
- **Taking it personally.** At a drive with a thousand people and a handful of
  roles, most people are rejected. That is the arithmetic, not a verdict on you.

## How to practise

Set up a Naukri alert and a LinkedIn search for "walk-in fresher Bangalore"
today. Pick one drive in the next two weeks. Go, whatever state you think your
preparation is in, and start the rejection log that evening.
