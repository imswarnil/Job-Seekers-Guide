Trains added a length to speed and distance. Boats add a current: the river
pushes you along one way and holds you back the other. Four formulas, and the
topic is done.

## Why it matters

Boats and streams appears in TCS, Infosys, Capgemini and AMCAT-style papers,
usually as one question when it shows up at all. It is one of the shortest
topics to learn (an evening) and one of the fastest to answer (30 seconds), so
it is pure profit.

Two words, defined once. **Downstream** means moving with the current.
**Upstream** means moving against it. "Speed in still water" is the boat's own
speed with no current.

## What to study

| # | Sub-type |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Given downstream and upstream speeds, find boat and stream speed |
| 2 | Given boat and stream speed, find time for a trip |
| 3 | A round trip taking a total time: find the distance |
| 4 | "Upstream takes twice as long": find the ratio of speeds |

## The formulas

```text
b = speed of boat in still water
s = speed of stream

Downstream speed  u = b + s
Upstream speed    v = b − s

Boat speed    b = (u + v) / 2
Stream speed  s = (u − v) / 2
```

::tip
Everything on this page is those four lines. If a question gives you distances
and times, turn them into `u` and `v` first, then use the last two lines.
::

## A worked example

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="Recover both speeds" icon="i-lucide-sailboat"}
  **A boat goes 24 km downstream in 2 hours and 12 km upstream in 2 hours. Find
  the boat's speed in still water and the stream's speed.**

  ```text
  u = 24 ÷ 2 = 12 km/h
  v = 12 ÷ 2 = 6 km/h
  b = (12 + 6) / 2 = 9 km/h
  s = (12 − 6) / 2 = 3 km/h
  ```

  **Answer: boat 9 km/h, stream 3 km/h.**
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="The ratio question" icon="i-lucide-divide"}
  **Rowing a distance upstream takes twice as long as rowing it downstream.
  Find the ratio of boat speed to stream speed.**

  Twice the time means half the speed, so `v = u / 2`.

  ```text
  b − s = (b + s) / 2
  2b − 2s = b + s
  b = 3s
  ```

  **Answer: 3 : 1.** General shortcut: if upstream takes `k` times as long,
  `b : s = (k + 1) : (k − 1)`. Here `(2 + 1) : (2 − 1) = 3 : 1`.
  :::
::

## Common mistakes

- **Swapping upstream and downstream.** Downstream is always the faster one.
- **Using the boat's still-water speed for a trip.** A trip is always either
  `u` or `v`, never `b`.
- **Averaging `u` and `v` for a round trip's average speed.** It is the same
  trap as the last page: use total distance over total time.

## Practice questions

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="1. Downstream 16 km/h, upstream 10 km/h. Boat and stream?"}
  **Boat 13 km/h, stream 3 km/h.**
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="2. Boat 15 km/h, stream 3 km/h. Time for 36 km downstream?"}
  **2 hours.** `36 ÷ 18`.
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="3. A man rows 30 km downstream in 3 hours and 20 km upstream in 4 hours. His speed in still water?"}
  **7.5 km/h.** `u = 10`, `v = 5`, `b = 7.5`.
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="4. Boat 8 km/h, stream 2 km/h. A round trip takes 4 hours. How far away is the place?"}
  **15 km.** `d/10 + d/6 = 4`, so `8d/30 = 4`, `d = 15`.
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="5. Upstream takes three times as long as downstream. Ratio of boat to stream speed?"}
  **2 : 1.** `(3 + 1) : (3 − 1) = 4 : 2`.
  :::
::
