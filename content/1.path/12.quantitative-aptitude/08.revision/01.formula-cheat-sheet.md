You have been through every topic: numbers, arithmetic, time, counting,
geometry and data interpretation. This page puts every formula from those
chapters in one place, so that revision is one page and not thirty.

## Why it matters

The night before a drive, and the morning of it on the bus, you do not have
time to reopen thirty pages. You need the formulas and the traps, and nothing
else. Copy this page into your own notebook, in your own handwriting, and read
it in the queue outside every walk-in. Writing it out once is half the
revision.

## What to study

- [ ] Read each section and **cover the right-hand side**. Say the formula, then check.
- [ ] Mark every formula you could not produce. Those are tomorrow's revision.
- [ ] Copy the whole page by hand once. Writing it is how it sticks.
- [ ] Read **the four traps** at the end on the morning of every test.

## Numbers

```text [Numbers]
Divisible by 3 / 9     sum of digits divisible by 3 / 9
Divisible by 4 / 8     last 2 / last 3 digits divisible by 4 / 8
Divisible by 11        alternating sum of digits is 0 or a multiple of 11
Unit digit of a power  cycles of 4: 2(2,4,8,6) 3(3,9,7,1) 7(7,9,3,1) 8(8,4,2,6)
Number of factors      N = a^p b^q c^r  ->  (p+1)(q+1)(r+1)
Trailing zeros in n!   n/5 + n/25 + n/125 + ...
Sum 1 to n             n(n+1)/2
Sum of first n odd     n^2
Sum of squares 1 to n  n(n+1)(2n+1)/6
HCF x LCM              = product of the two numbers (two numbers only)
Same remainder         HCF of the differences
Remainder r each time  LCM + r
Indices                a^m a^n = a^(m+n)   a^m/a^n = a^(m-n)   (a^m)^n = a^(mn)
                       a^0 = 1   a^(-n) = 1/a^n   a^(m/n) = (nth root of a)^m
BODMAS                 brackets, orders, then ÷ and x left to right, then + and - left to right
```

## Arithmetic

```text [Percentages]
Percentage change      (new - old) / old x 100
Successive a% and b%   a + b + ab/100   (use -b for a fall)
Up r%, then to restore needs a fall of   r / (100 + r) x 100
Price up r%, spend same: cut consumption by   r / (100 + r) x 100
```

```text [Profit and loss]
Profit %               profit / CP x 100      (always on cost price)
SP                     CP x (100 + gain%) / 100    or  CP x (100 - loss%) / 100
Discount               on marked price: SP = MP x (100 - d%) / 100
Successive discounts   a + b - ab/100
Same SP, x% gain on one, x% loss on the other   net loss = x^2/100 %
False weight           gain % = error / (true value - error) x 100
```

```text [Interest]
Simple interest        SI = P x R x T / 100
Amount (compound)      A = P (1 + R/100)^T      CI = A - P
Half-yearly            rate R/2, time 2T        Quarterly: R/4, 4T
CI - SI, 2 years       P (R/100)^2
CI - SI, 3 years       P (R/100)^2 (3 + R/100)
```

```text [Ratio, partnership, averages, ages, mixtures]
a:b and b:c            make b equal in both, then read a:b:c
Mean proportional      root(ab)     Third proportional  b^2/a
Fourth proportional of a, b, c     bc/a
Partnership            profit shared in ratio capital x time
Average                sum / count      sum = average x count
New member changes average by d    new value = old average + (n+1) x d
Average of 1 to n      (n + 1) / 2
Ages                   write present ages as x; "n years ago" is x - n for everybody
Alligation             cheaper : dearer = (dearer - mean) : (mean - cheaper)
Repeated replacement   left = initial x (1 - x/V)^n
```

```text [Time and work, pipes]
Work rate              done in n days -> 1/n of the job per day
Together               A in a days, B in b days: ab / (a + b) days
Men, days, hours       M1 D1 H1 / W1 = M2 D2 H2 / W2
Pipes                  filling pipe +1/n, leak or outlet -1/n, then add rates
```

```text [Speed, trains, boats]
km/h to m/s            x 5/18        m/s to km/h   x 18/5
Distance               speed x time
Average speed          total distance / total time
Equal distances at x and y          2xy / (x + y)
Relative speed         opposite directions: add;  same direction: subtract
Train passes a pole or person       its length / its speed
Train passes a platform             (train + platform) / speed
Two trains, opposite                (L1 + L2) / (s1 + s2)
Two trains, same direction          (L1 + L2) / (s1 - s2)
Boats                  downstream = b + s,  upstream = b - s
```

## Counting

```text [Permutations, combinations, probability]
n!                     n x (n-1) x ... x 1,   0! = 1
Arrangements nPr       n! / (n - r)!
Selections nCr         n! / (r! (n - r)!)        nCr = nC(n-r)
Round a table          (n - 1)!
Letters with repeats   n! / (p! q! ...)
Probability            favourable / total
Not A                  1 - P(A)
A or B                 P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B)
A and B, independent   P(A) x P(B)
Totals                 one die 6, two dice 36, coin tossed n times 2^n, cards 52 (4 suits of 13)
```

## A worked example: the four traps

These four decide more marks than any formula above, because each has a wrong
answer placed in the options on purpose.

::steps
### Percentage point is not percentage
A pass rate goes from 68% to 76%. That is a rise of **8 percentage points**,
and a rise of 8 / 68 x 100 = about **11.8 per cent**. Both appear as options.

### Average speed is not the average of speeds
Bangalore to Mysore, about 150 km, at 50 km/h and back at 75 km/h. Average
speed is 2 x 50 x 75 / (50 + 75) = 7,500 / 125 = **60 km/h**, not 62.5.
Check: 3 hours out, 2 hours back, 300 km in 5 hours.

### Largest increase is not fastest growth
A department goes from 20 to 30 students (+10, 50%). Another goes from 200 to
240 (+40, 20%). The second had the largest increase, the first grew fastest.

### Profit is on cost price
Bought at 400, sold at 500. Profit is 100 / 400 = **25%**, not 100 / 500 =
20%. Discount, on the other hand, is on the marked price.
::

## Common mistakes

- **Revising by reading only.** Cover the formula and produce it. Reading
  feels like revision and is not.
- **Learning a formula without its condition.** HCF x LCM = product is for
  two numbers. The infinite GP sum needs r between -1 and 1. The condition is
  where the trap is.
- **Carrying formulas for topics you will skip.** If you have decided logarithms
  are a pass-three topic, do not spend the morning of the test on them.

## How to practise

- **Days 25–30 of the plan:** read this page every morning, covered.
- **After every mock:** find the formula behind each wrong answer on this page
  and circle it.
- **The morning of a test:** the four traps, and the formulas you circled.
  Nothing new.

## Practice questions

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="1. A price rises 20% and then falls 20%. What is the net change?"}
  **A 4% fall.** 20 - 20 + (20 x -20)/100 = -4.
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="2. What is CI - SI on 10,000 at 10% for 2 years?"}
  **100.** 10,000 x (10/100)^2 = 100.
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="3. What is the angle between the hands at 3:40?"}
  **130 degrees.** |30 x 3 - 5.5 x 40| = |90 - 220| = 130.
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="4. What is the sum of the AP 3, 7, 11, ... to 10 terms?"}
  **210.** 10/2 x (6 + 9 x 4) = 5 x 42 = 210.
  :::
  :::accordion-item{label="5. In how many ways can 5 people sit round a table?"}
  **24.** (5 - 1)! = 24.
  :::
::
