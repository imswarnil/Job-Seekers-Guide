Every other track in this guide tells you what to do. This one tells you what I
did, including the parts I would do differently, so you can check the advice
against a real life instead of taking it on trust.

I started writing things down in 2018, in a PG in BTM Layout, after a handful of
interviews had shown me that I knew nothing. I promised myself something then:
if I ever got a job, this record would become a path for the next person in my
position. You are reading that promise. The guide is the path. This track is the
record it came from.

::pull-quote
I did not clear a single written round in four years of college. If you are
worried that you are behind, I was behind you.
::

It is not a hero story. The struggles are the point, because they are the part
you are living now, and they are the part nobody posts.

## The whole route

::timeline{label="From Mahroni to Europe" caption="Every chapter below is one stretch of this line."}
  :::timeline-item{title="Kota, then LNCT Bhopal" date="2013–2018" state="done"}
  Did not clear IIT. Four years of computer science. Never cleared a written round.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{title="The masked channel" date="2017" state="done"}
  A YouTube channel nobody at home knew about. About a thousand subscribers.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{title="The kitchen table" date="2018" state="done"}
  My father wanted a government teacher. My sister said Bangalore.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{title="The train, and BTM Layout" date="2018" state="done"}
  Mahroni to Bangalore. A PG, a few interviews, and the discovery that I knew nothing.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{title="JSpiders" date="2018" state="done"}
  Three days of demo classes everywhere, then about three months of Java, SQL and web.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{title="33 walk-ins" date="2018–2019" state="done"}
  Rejected at every one. Selected at the 34th, one of 700 to 1000 candidates.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{title="The first job" date="2019" state="done"}
  A startup. 1.8 LPA, ₹13,000 a month, a bond, six days a week.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{title="Accenture" date="2019–2022" state="done"}
  Selected two months later. Salesforce, not web development. Three years.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{title="The switch" date="2022" state="done"}
  A friend on 21 LPA. Five offers, best one 15.5 LPA. Cognizant.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{title="Twilio" date="2023" state="done"}
  Left Cognizant after nine months. PwC and Twilio. Twilio at 30+ LPA, Salesforce analytics in GTM.
  :::
  :::timeline-item{title="Education First, Europe" date="Now" state="current"}
  After three years and more at Twilio, a sponsored visa, and this guide.
  :::
::

## How to read it

Read it in order. Each chapter is short, ten minutes or less, and each ends with
what that stretch taught me in a form you can use, plus a link to the part of
the guide that does the teaching.

::note
Where you see a grey box waiting for a photograph or a clip, it is a real one I
have and have not put up yet. Nothing in these chapters is invented to make it
read better. Where I do not remember a number, I say so.
::
