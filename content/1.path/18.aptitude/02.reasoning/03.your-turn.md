Two reasoning shapes learned. Fifteen questions, fifteen minutes, and the same
rule as before: paper, pen, and a mark against anything that has not resolved in
fifteen seconds.

## Series

1. `5, 9, 17, 33, 65, …`
2. `240, 120, 40, 10, …`
3. `7, 10, 16, 28, 52, …`
4. `A, C, F, J, O, …`
5. Odd one out: `121, 144, 169, 180, 196`

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="Answers 1 to 5" icon="i-lucide-check"}
  **1.** `× 2 − 1` → **129**. Check: `5×2−1=9`, `9×2−1=17`. Fits every gap.

  **2.** Divided by 2, then 3, then 4, so next is ÷ 5 → **2**.

  **3.** Differences 3, 6, 12, 24, doubling. Next difference 48 → **100**.

  **4.** Positions 1, 3, 6, 10, 15: gaps of 2, 3, 4, 5. Next gap 6 → position 21
  = **U**.

  **5.** 121, 144, 169 and 196 are `11², 12², 13², 14²`. **180** is not a square.
  :::
::

## Syllogisms

For each, does the conclusion follow?

6. **All students are members. Some members are athletes.**
   *Conclusion:* Some students are athletes.
7. **No lecturer is an administrator. All administrators are employees.**
   *Conclusion:* Some employees are not lecturers.
8. **All applicants submitted documents. Ravi submitted documents.**
   *Conclusion:* Ravi is an applicant.

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="Answers 6 to 8" icon="i-lucide-check"}
  **6. Does not follow.** The athletes could all be among the members who are
  not students. One consistent drawing where it fails is enough.

  **7. Follows.** Administrators are employees, and no administrator is a
  lecturer, so those employees are not lecturers. As long as at least one
  administrator exists, some employees are not lecturers.

  **8. Does not follow.** This is affirming the consequent, and it is the most
  common logical error there is. All applicants submitted documents; that says
  nothing about whether everyone who submitted documents is an applicant. A
  member of staff could have submitted documents.

  Question 8 is worth dwelling on, because the same mistake in a job sounds
  like: "every time the server crashed, memory was high. Memory is high, so the
  server is about to crash."
  :::
::

## Arrangements

9. Six people sit around a circular table **facing the centre**: P, Q, R, S, T
   and U. R is second to the left of P. Q is between S and T. U is opposite S.
   Who is to the immediate right of P?

10. Five books are stacked. The Physics book is directly above Chemistry.
    Maths is at the bottom. Biology is directly below English. Chemistry is
    above Biology. What is the order from top?

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="Answers 9 and 10" icon="i-lucide-check"}
  **9.** Facing the centre, so from your view looking down, their left is
  anticlockwise. Draw six positions.

  Place P at the top. "R is second to the left of P" means two positions
  anticlockwise. U opposite S fixes a diameter. Q between S and T constrains the
  rest.

  Working through: the arrangement is **P, T, Q, S, R, U** clockwise, and the
  person to P's immediate right is **U**.

  The mark is lost by treating left as clockwise. Write "facing centre: left is
  anticlockwise" at the top of your drawing before placing anybody.

  **10.** Work from the fixed point. Maths is at the bottom, position 5.
  Chemistry is above Biology, Biology is directly below English, and Physics is
  directly above Chemistry.

  So: `English, Biology` is a pair, and `Physics, Chemistry` is a pair, with
  Chemistry above Biology.

  From the top: **Physics, Chemistry, English, Biology, Maths**. Check: Physics
  directly above Chemistry, yes. Biology directly below English, yes. Chemistry
  above Biology, yes. Maths at the bottom, yes.
  :::
::

## Relations

11. "Pointing to a woman, Ravi said: 'She is the daughter of my grandfather's
    only son.'" How is she related to Ravi?
12. "P is the sister of Q. R is the mother of Q. S is the father of R. T is the
    mother of S." How is P related to T?
13. A man said: "That boy's mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is
    the boy related to the man?

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="Answers 11 to 13" icon="i-lucide-check"}
  **11.** Grandfather's only son is Ravi's father. His daughter is Ravi's
  **sister**.

  **12.** P and Q are siblings, R is their mother, S is R's father so P's
  grandfather, T is S's mother so P's **great-grandmother**.

  **13.** "The only daughter of my mother" is the man's sister, unless the man
  has no sisters, in which case his mother's only daughter cannot be himself. So
  the boy's mother is the man's sister, and the boy is his **nephew**.

  The trap: answering "son" by reading "only daughter of my mother" as the
  speaker. The speaker is a man. Circle the gendered words as you read.
  :::
::

## Coding and direction

14. If `COLLEGE` is written as `DPMMFHF`, how is `STUDENT` written?
15. A student walks 4 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, turns right again
    and walks 4 km. How far is she from the start, and in which direction?

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="Answers 14 and 15" icon="i-lucide-check"}
  **14.** Each letter moves forward one. `S→T, T→U, U→V, D→E, E→F, N→O, T→U` =
  **TUVEFOU**.

  This is why you write the alphabet with positions in the margin at the start.
  With it, this is eight seconds; without it, thirty and error-prone.

  **15.** North 4, then right is east 3, then right is south 4. The north and
  south cancel, leaving 3 km **east**.

  Draw it. Three lines on paper, and the answer is visible. Attempted mentally,
  the second right turn is where people go wrong.
  :::
::

## What your score means

| Score | Read it as |
| --- | --- |
| 13 to 15 | Solid. Keep the drawing habit and move on |
| 9 to 12 | One category is weak. Look at which, it is usually arrangements |
| Under 9 | Reread the two lessons. This section rewards method more than any other |

::note
If you got the series questions right and the arrangements wrong, that is the
normal pattern, and it is not about ability. Series can be done in the head.
Arrangements cannot, and people who are good at mental arithmetic are the most
likely to try.
::

## The habit to take away

::pull-quote
Reasoning questions are not harder than arithmetic ones. They are harder to do
in your head, which is a completely different problem and has a completely
different fix.
::
