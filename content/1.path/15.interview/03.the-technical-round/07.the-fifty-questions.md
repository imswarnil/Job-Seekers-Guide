These are the fifty technical questions I was asked most, and heard asked most,
across fresher interviews in Bangalore: service companies, mid-size firms and
startups. Not every interview asks all fifty. Almost every interview asks some.

## Why it matters

The list is short because the questions repeat. The same interviewers read the
same question banks, and the same core subjects are on every fresher job
description. If you can answer these fifty in the five-step format, you are ready
for most fresher technical rounds.

## What to do

::steps
### Tick the ones you can answer now
Out loud, in the five steps, in under two minutes. Not "I have read about it".

### Revise the rest from the linked track
Each group links to the track that teaches it.

### Three a day, out loud, until the list is done
About two and a half weeks. Record one a day.
::

## A worked example

Question 27, in the five steps, in under a minute:

> **Definition.** "All three remove data, at different levels." **How it
> works.** "DELETE removes rows, can have a WHERE, and can be rolled back.
> TRUNCATE removes every row at once, is much faster, and usually cannot be
> rolled back. DROP removes the whole table, structure and all." **Example.**
> "`DELETE FROM exam_result WHERE marks IS NULL` removes unmarked rows only.
> `TRUNCATE TABLE exam_result` empties the table. `DROP TABLE exam_result` means
> the table no longer exists." **Trade-off.** "DELETE for anything selective or
> anything I might need to undo. TRUNCATE only when I really mean all of it."
> **Stop.**

::warning{icon="i-lucide-alert-triangle"}
Rollback behaviour of TRUNCATE differs between databases: in PostgreSQL it can
be rolled back inside a transaction, in MySQL and Oracle it cannot. If an
interviewer pushes on it, saying "it depends on the database, and in MySQL it
cannot" is a better answer than a flat rule.
::

## Common mistakes

- **Memorising one-line answers from a PDF.** The one-liner is step 1 of 5.
  Interviewers ask "how?" next, every time.
- **Skipping the subjects you find boring.** Networks and OS are the most
  skipped and are asked in almost every service company interview.
- **Only reading.** You have not learned an answer until you have said it out
  loud without looking.

## How to practise

Three questions a day, out loud, in the five steps, timed at under two minutes
each. Tick them here as you go. In the last three days before an interview, go
through all fifty once, one line each, as a warm-up.
